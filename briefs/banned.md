# Banned list

The defaults that make a page look AI-generated. Every teardown in `sites/` is checked against this list, and so is anything built from a brief.

A pattern can be fine when the subject really calls for it (a live radio's pulsing LIVE dot, mono caps on a mono typeface's specimen). If you use one, say why in the brief.

## Type

- Inter or Geist as the default face.
- Tiny letter-spaced uppercase labels above headings (the "kicker").
- Monospace caps for labels and nav, like `////// MANIFESTO` or `PROJECT_02`.
- Tags chained with middots or bullets: `DESIGN • WEB • 3D`.

## Colour and surface

- Tailwind grays and slate as the palette.
- Glass nav pills and frosted panels.
- Glowing outline pills and glow cards.
- Beams, spotlights and shimmer borders.

## Layout

- A centered hero, then three cards.
- Pill-framed numbers and badges on every card.

## Motion

- Fade-up on every section as it scrolls in.
- Pulsing dot badges.
- Smooth-scroll hijacking, unless the whole page is one scroll-driven scene.

## Words

- Clever lines and puns in place of saying what the thing is.
- A joke on every line.
